## The problem
The retention team contacted customers at random, wasting budget and annoying loyal users.

## What I did
Built a gradient-boosted model on purchase and support history, then ranked customers by predicted risk and expected value. Added SHAP explanations so the marketing team could see why each customer was flagged.

## The result
Targeted campaigns cut churn by 18% in the first quarter, with 30% less outreach.
