## The problem

The retention team contacted customers at random, wasting budget and annoying loyal users.

## What I did

Built a gradient-boosted model on purchase and support history, then ranked customers by predicted risk and expected value. Added SHAP explanations for the marketing team.

## The result

Targeted campaigns cut churn by 18% in the first quarter with 30% less outreach.
